FAQ

Is there a long contract?

No. Plans are month to month. There's no minimum term and no cancellation fee — I'd rather earn the renewal than lock you in.

How do I cancel?

Just email me. No forms, no phone trees. You keep all backups and access details, and I'll help with a clean handover to your next person or provider if you want one.

How quickly do you respond?

I work a 4-on/4-off night-shift rota, so replies come within 24h on my off-days and within 48h on shift days. That's the honest promise on every plan — the Plus plan means your work is first in the queue on each reply day, not that I've invented a faster reply time. If your site is down or showing a security problem, it's treated as urgent on every plan and handled in my next reply window.

Do you host my site?

No — your site stays on your own hosting, in your name. That means you're never tied to me. If your host is a poor fit, I can recommend options and handle the move.
